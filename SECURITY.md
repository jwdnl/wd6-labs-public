# Security and privacy reports

Report vulnerabilities, accidental data exposure or abuse involving WD6 Labs
to [abuse@wd6.net](mailto:abuse@wd6.net).

Please include the affected public hostname or repository path, the time of
observation and enough detail to reproduce the issue. Do not include Reticulum
private identity material, credentials or unrelated personal data in a report.

The gateway is experimental and has no SLA, but security and privacy reports
are reviewed as operational incidents.
